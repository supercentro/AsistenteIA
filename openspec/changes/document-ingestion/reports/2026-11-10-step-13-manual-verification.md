# Group 13: manual verification with the real model

Branch `feature/document-ingestion`, HEAD `8dd9de5` at the start. Tasks 13.1 and 13.2, executed on an **isolated disposable database** (see "Deviation from the task text"). Migration `0005` was **not** applied to the development database. No group after 13 was run.

## Deviation from the task text
Tasks 13.1 and 13.2 say to back up the development database, apply `0005` to it, test there and restore it to `0005`. The authorisation of this group forbids applying `0005` to `asistenteia` and requires isolated databases. The verification was therefore done on a throwaway PostgreSQL 15 + pgvector container created for it and removed afterwards. Everything the tasks check was checked (HTTP flows, real model, Spanish search, time and memory), except two items that only make sense on the development database: the backup/restore of `asistenteia` and `alembic current` = `0005` there. Those two remain for the group that is authorised to touch it. For that reason **13.1 and 13.2 are not ticked in `tasks.md`** (the text is unchanged; changing it needs the user's decision).

## 13.2 Search with the real model (functional checks, not quality metrics)
The search is the repository method `ChunkRepository.find_similar` (there is no search endpoint in this change), called with the real embedder from a container on the internal network.

- Five Spanish questions (derivative, French Revolution, photosynthesis, stack, guillotine). **For the enrolled student and for the owner the top-1 passage contained an expected term in 5 of 5**, results ordered by decreasing cosine similarity (0.58 to 0.81 for the top hit). This is a functional smoke check of an end-to-end pipeline on a 4-topic corpus, **not** a measure of semantic quality: the corpus is tiny, synthetic and written by the same author as the questions. The quality figures of the spike (Hit@1 0.689, Hit@3 0.889, MRR 0.798) stay the only quality claim, and the evaluation with real material is change 12.
- A student that is not enrolled, another teacher, a student presenting the teacher role and an unknown user all got **0 results** for every question.
- A document in `processing` (the 1,649-chunk one, searched while it was being processed) and the `failed` documents were not returned; chunks of non-`ready` documents: **0**.
- Search latency per query after the query embedding: 3 to 40 ms.
- Delete: the 19 and 1,649 chunks of the two deleted documents went to 0; orphan chunks 0, orphan jobs 0, leftover `payload` bytes 0.

## Security and isolation observed
- The worker has no route outside: a TCP connection to `1.1.1.1:443` failed and `huggingface.co` did not resolve; the offline variables were `1`. The 16 `realmodel` tests were also run with `--network none`: 16 passed. **No download happened.**

- Logs: 23 worker lines and 365 API lines were reviewed. They contain identifiers, job outcomes and codes only: **no** document text (searched for 9 distinctive words of the corpus), **no** emails, passwords, tokens, vectors or URLs.

## Risks and limits of this verification
1. 13.1/13.2 are verified on a disposable database, not on `asistenteia`; the two development-database items remain open (see the deviation).
2. The corpus is synthetic and small; nothing here measures retrieval quality.
3. The 2 GiB stream was rejected as `CORRUPT_FILE` by the parser before it expanded, so the memory limit of the PDF child was not triggered by it in this run; that limit is proved by the child tests of groups 6 and 12.
4. Time and memory are for one worker, one document at a time, a 4-CPU quota and a 420 KB text; they are not a capacity test.
5. The monitor samples every 0.3 s: a transaction shorter than that could be missed, but one that lasted during inference (tens of seconds) could not.
6. Dependabot alert #1 remains open (tasks 15.1 and 17.3). The seven untracked reference documents were not touched.
