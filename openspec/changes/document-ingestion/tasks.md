Reports required by `docs/openspec-tasks-mandatory-steps.md` are saved in `openspec/changes/document-ingestion/reports/`. Every group writes its failing tests first (recorded in `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-tdd-evidence.md`) and lands its own tests and documentation.
